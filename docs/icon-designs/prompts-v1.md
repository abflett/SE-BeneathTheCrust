# Working Knowledge Icon Prompts - Version 1

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The five reference images, in order, were the supplied progression-tree screenshot followed by the vanilla Datapad, Basic Assembler, Grinder, and Lab Equipment icons.

## Shared Direction

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Space Engineers G-menu/inventory icon concept
Input images: Image 1 is the user's progression-tree screenshot and is the primary in-game context reference. Image 2 is the vanilla Datapad item icon. Images 3-5 are vanilla Space Engineers block icons (Basic Assembler, Grinder, Lab Equipment) and define the rendering language.
Style/medium: Match the vanilla Space Engineers icon language visible in the references: a single isolated utilitarian object, simple 3/4 orthographic view, compact silhouette, subdued pale blue-gray monochrome materials, low saturation, soft diffuse shading, restrained edge highlights, modest ambient occlusion, sparse surface detail, readable at 64x64. It should look like it belongs beside vanilla block icons in the dark progression tree.
Composition: one centered object only, generous padding, plain transparent-looking black background, no frame or tile.
Color palette: cold desaturated steel blue-gray, nearly monochrome. No strong color accents.
Constraints: visually consistent with the actual vanilla icon references; functional industrial object; distinct silhouette; no text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, UI overlay, border, surrounding panel, scenery, or multiple objects.
Avoid: glossy concept-art rendering, neon cyan, orange lights, holograms, glowing circuit diagrams, elaborate sci-fi frames, cinematic lighting, photorealism, fantasy, excessive detail.
```

## Data Fragment

```text
Primary request: Design a Data Fragment icon representing incomplete salvaged research data. Show a small broken physical datapad/data wafer fragment with one visibly missing or fractured portion and a simple incomplete etched circuit path. It must remain an intact collectible object, not floating debris. Make it flatter, duller, simpler, and more monochrome than the prior glowing concept.
```

## Schematic Family Unlocker

```text
Primary request: Design a hidden Schematic Family Unlocker icon representing access to a whole related family of engineering knowledge. Show a compact physical archive/index datapad or rugged catalog cartridge with a subtle branching/stacked-card relief embossed into its face. The branching motif must be shallow physical engraving, not a glowing screen. Keep the silhouette clearly different from the broken fragment and complete exact schematic.
```

## Exact Data Schematic

```text
Primary request: Design an Exact Data Schematic icon representing one complete durable engineering schematic. Show an intact sealed physical datapad/blueprint cartridge with a simple complete etched technical line pattern and a closed, orderly casing. No depicted machine or specific block. It should look complete and precise without a checkmark, bright screen, or emissive glow.
```
