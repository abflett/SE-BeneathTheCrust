[h1]Working Knowledge[/h1]
[h3]A salvage-first progression overhaul for Space Engineers.[/h3]

Working Knowledge transforms Space Engineers survival into a true field-engineering experience.

Instead of racing through a linear build-one-block menu, you must recover schematic data, decode research fragments, and practice your trade. Wrecks, captured ships, abandoned stations, and POIs are no longer just component scrap heaps—they are critical opportunities to study unfamiliar tech and advance your capabilities.

Every find presents a real tactical decision:
[list]
[*]Keep the block functional and build around it?
[*]Relocate it using specialized tools?
[*]Strip it carefully for parts?
[*]Or grind it down for raw research progress and accept the loss?
[/list]

[b]Research[/b] unlocks what you understand.
[b]Proficiency[/b] determines how well you build, repair, and salvage.

[h2]Core Systems[/h2]
[h3]Tailored Schematic Families[/h3]
Vanilla progression chains are replaced by grouped, tech-logical schematic families. Most public cube blocks belong to a family that must be researched before you can construct it from scratch.

[h3]Field Research[/h3]
Gain progression points by dismantling unknown technologies, decoding rare Data Fragments from loot, or securing exact Data Schematics from POIs, missions, scenarios, and admin events. Unlocking a family opens its technology for normal production.

[h3]Practical Proficiency[/h3]
Unlocking a schematic is only half the battle. Your hands-on experience dictates your efficiency:
[list]
[*]Low Proficiency: Slower work, higher chance of component loss during salvage, higher repair costs, and potential construction setbacks.
[*]High Proficiency: Clean salvage, faster builds, reliable repairs, and peak component recovery.
[/list]
[i]Technology isn't just unlocked—it's mastered.[/i]

[h2]Recommended Gameplay Setup[/h2]
Working Knowledge is fully functional as a standalone mod, but the physical salvage loop shines brightest when paired with tools that encourage moving and preserving machinery.

[h3]Required & Recommended Companions[/h3]
[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1965654081]Rich HUD Master[/url] (Required)[/b]
Provides the core engine for the graphical settings menu and live progress overlay overlay.

[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=510790477]Advanced Welding[/url] (Recommended)[/b]
Allows detaching and reattaching intact functional blocks, making intact salvage vastly more valuable than raw scrap.

[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2891367014]Improvised Experimentation[/url] (Recommended)[/b]
Adds physics-based dragging, carrying, and repositioning, turning heavy wreck salvage into an engaging spatial puzzle.

[h2]Configuration & Commands[/h2]
The fastest way to configure your settings, adjust difficulty presets, or customize progress bars is through the graphical settings panel. 

Open the GUI in-game by typing:
[code]/wk settings[/code]

For a general overview or a full list of available fallback chat commands, use:
[code]/wk
/wk help[/code]

[h2]Compatibility Notes[/h2]
Working Knowledge alters block unlocks, grinding output, salvage rates, and placement rules. Mods that alter these same mechanics should be tested together.

[h3]Custom Block Compatibility Layers[/h3]
Custom block mods can be connected to Working Knowledge using small layer mods. The included Layer Toolkit scans third-party block mods and generates the mappings, research definitions, and exact Data Schematics required for custom groups.

Admins can use [b]/wk admin audit[/b] to check loaded layers. Detailed compatibility warnings also appear in the Space Engineers log and F11 mod-error screen.
